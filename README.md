Every day, thousands of automated malicious bots register on PrestaShop e-commerce stores. They flood your database with:
- **Gibberish customer registrations** (e.g. `VqSHetLFVX QamXlXwTWe`, random CamelCase).
- **Spam URLs in names** (e.g. `Hot Helena Waiting www.xurl.es/...`).
- **Phishing and disposable email domains** (`.ru`, `.xyz`, `.top`, `.click`, mailinator, yopmail).
- **Orphan postal addresses and abandoned carts** consuming gigabytes of database storage and slowing down checkout and SQL queries.
- **Contact form advertising and phishing messages** polluting your customer support inbox.

**SpamCustomerCleaner** is the all-in-one professional solution developed by **Genisoft web** to diagnose, clean, and permanently protect your store.

### 🛡️ 10-Point Heuristic Detection Rules
1. **URLs & Hyperlinks**: Detects domain extensions and `http/www` injected into first and last names.
2. **Advertising Keywords**: Detects spam phrases (`dating`, `wants to meet`, `seo-package`, `casino`, etc.).
3. **Random Mixed Casing**: Flags bot-generated CamelCase patterns (`GJfwUoNDU`, `oDglEQIqBVVMI`).
4. **Consecutive Consonants**: Flags unpronounceable consonant clusters (5+ consecutive consonants).
5. **Vowel Ratio Analysis**: Detects strings with abnormal vowel frequencies (< 17% on 5+ letter words).
6. **Atypical Name Starts**: Detects bot prefix generators (`Brkjj`, `Vkues`, `Qcpmu`).
7. **Identical Name/Surname**: Detects fake matching first and last names.
8. **Disposable & Temporary Domains**: Flags temporary inbox providers (`yopmail`, `10minutemail`, `trashmail`, etc.).
9. **High-Risk TLDs**: Detects malicious top-level domains (`.ru`, `.su`, `.xyz`, `.top`, `.click`, `.loan`, `.date`).
10. **Algorithmic Email Patterns**: Flags usernames generated by automated software (`[consonants][numbers]@domain`).

### 🔒 100% Safety Guarantee (Zero False Positives)
- **Order Protection Barrier**: Any customer who has ever placed at least one real order in `ps_orders` is **categorically immune**. They will never be flagged, selected, or deleted under any circumstance.
- **Custom Whitelist**: Protect internal staff, administrator emails, or specific customer domains with a simple list.
- **Relational Integrity**: Clean relational cascades (`ps_customer`, `ps_address`, `ps_cart`, `ps_cart_product`, `ps_customer_thread`, `ps_customer_message`) preventing foreign key integrity errors and orphaned data.
